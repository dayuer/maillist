Questions

Is Nibwire available today?

The software runs today and the source is public. The hosted service opens to the early-access list first, in small batches, so every migration gets attention. Join and we'll email you when your spot opens.

Will my emails land in the inbox?

Deliverability comes from your domain's authentication and your list's health more than from the platform. Nibwire sends through Amazon SES, signs mail with your domain, and suppresses bounces and complaints automatically. When you move, we warm your domain up by sending to your most engaged readers first.

What license is Nibwire under?

Nibwire is free software under the GNU AGPL-3.0-only. Running it for your own newsletters, including commercially, is free. A commercial license is available if you want to offer a modified version as a service without publishing your changes, or embed Nibwire in closed-source software.

Can I leave?

Yes, and it's easy. Your data is one SQLite file you can download any time, and you can run that same file on your own server with the open binary.

What happens to the email I give you here?

We use it to tell you about early access and nothing else. It's stored on Nibwire itself — this waitlist runs on the product. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe.

I'm on Substack, Kit or Mailchimp. Does this work for me?

If you can export a CSV of subscribers, yes. Nibwire doesn't do paid subscriptions yet, so if most of your revenue is paid subs, wait for that.
