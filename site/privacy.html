Privacy

Last updated October 12, 2026.

What we collect

When you join the early-access list we store your email address and the time you signed up. Your IP address is used briefly to stop automated signups and is not stored with your address; our web server keeps standard access logs.

How we use it

To email you about Nibwire's early access and launch. We don't sell, rent or share your address, and we don't add you to anyone else's list.

Where it lives

The list is stored in a Nibwire database on our own server in the United States. Pages are served through Cloudflare. When we send email, it goes out through Amazon SES.

Your choices

Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. To have your address deleted entirely, reply to any email from us and ask.

Fonts

This site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when fetching them.
